## 7. Weak or non-specific signals
- Inconsistent with UIP pattern on HRCT (PPV only 22.7% for UIP, insufficient to distinguish) (1)
- Smoking history (ever vs never) - not selected by LASSO as significant predictor (1)
- Reticulation alone (non-specific fibrotic feature) (1)
- Honeycombing (part of definite UIP but excluded from this non-definitive cohort) (1)
- Inconsistent with UIP pattern on HRCT (PPV only 22.7% for UIP, insufficient to rule in or out) (1)
- Smoking history alone (not selected by LASSO as independent predictor) (1)
- Reticulation, traction bronchiectasis, honeycombing as general fibrotic features (not specific to UIP vs non-UIP in inconsistent-with-UIP subgroup) (1)
- HRCT pattern alone insufficient in low-prevalence populations (1)
- Total extent of pathological tissue (1)
- Total extent of GGO across whole lung (1)
- General reticulation without distribution specificity (1)
- Sex alone as classifier (1)
- Reticulation (present across all ILD subtypes) (1)
- Traction bronchiectasis (present in UIP and non-UIP fibrotic ILD) (1)
- Reduced FVC, TLC, DLCO (similar across all ILD groups) (1)
- Crackles at examination (nearly ubiquitous across all diagnoses: 88.7%) (1)
- Basilar zonal distribution (similar in UIP and non-UIP: 79.1% vs 80.9%) (1)
- Diffuse nodularity (not significantly associated with non-UIP, p=0.188) (1)
- Zonal distribution overall (not significantly different between UIP and non-UIP, p=0.546) (1)
- Digital clubbing (historically considered IPF-associated but evidence suggests limited discriminatory value) (1)
- MUC5B variant (also associated with fibrotic HP, RA-ILD; not IPF-specific) (1)
- Telomeropathy/short telomeres (also seen in fibrotic HP, CTD-ILD, unclassifiable ILD) (1)
- Familial fibrotic ILD (unclear how much it alters IPF probability specifically) (1)
- Restrictive physiology (general fibrotic ILD feature, not IPF-specific alone) (1)
- Probable UIP pattern on CT alone (66% PPV for IPF, 34% are non-IPF) (1)
- Histopathological UIP pattern (83% PPV for IPF diagnosis, not 100%) (1)
- Age (not significantly different between IPF and non-IPF groups) (1)
- PaO2 (not significantly different between IPF and non-IPF with probable UIP) (1)
- Traction bronchiectasis alone (present in both IPF and non-IPF) (1)
- Shortness of breath (87.5% - nonspecific across ILD types) (1)

## 8. Practical interpretation
These papers should be treated as the strongest direct evidence base for downstream IPF-vs-Other classification.
However:
- General fibrotic ILD abnormality is not the same as IPF-specific evidence.
- Radiomics and clinical variables should be interpreted jointly.
- Non-specific fibrosis signals should not be over-weighted.
