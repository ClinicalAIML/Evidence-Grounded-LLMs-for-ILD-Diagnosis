# Clinical Feature Dictionary for IPF vs Other

This file summarizes clinical variables reported as useful for distinguishing IPF from Other ILD.

## General principles
- Clinical variables should be interpreted jointly with imaging/radiomics evidence.
- Demographics, pulmonary function, comorbidities, biomarkers, and other variables may contribute to subtype discrimination.
- Clinical associations may be cohort-dependent and not always IPF-specific.
